# Scenarios

## Standard Starts

### Evacuee
You have survived the initial wave of panic, and have achieved (relative) safety in one of the many government evac shelters. The default start. Safe-ish building, basic supplies nearby, one NPC. The tutorial scenario without being called one.

### Friends to the End
You and your friends always talked about what you'd do when the world ended... now it's come true, and you've made your way to a remote cabin your friend Liam always said to head for. It's been a hell of a journey. You hope Liam and the others made it in one piece. Start at a cabin with NPC companions. Social survival — you have allies but more mouths to feed.

### Helicopter Crash
While being transported to a different military base, the pilot lost control of the helicopter and crashed in the middle of nowhere. You seem to be the only survivor. Crash site in wilderness. Limited professions (military context). Possible injuries. Start with wreckage to scavenge.

### Infected
In the chaos and panic of the evacuation, you got bitten by something! You didn't get proper medical care, and now the wound has started turning green. Starts with active zombie infection. Race against time to find antibiotics or accept your fate.

### Prison
When the Cataclysm happened, you were convicted or working in a prison. Now the inmates and guards alike have turned into mindless horrors and joined forces... you might need to expedite your escape plans. Start inside a prison. Locked doors, zombie inmates/guards, limited gear. Must escape the building first.

### Sheltered
When the apocalypse broke out, you were evacuated to a small underground bunker. Here you have lived, never leaving, lest the dangers of the unknown find you. Supplies are running low, and for the first time since the Cataclysm, you will be forced to face the outside world. LMOE bunker start. Good initial supplies but they're finite. The comfort zone must be abandoned.

### Surrounded
You've attracted the attention of living dead in some way, now they're all around you and you'll likely have to fight through them if you want to escape. Immediate combat. Zombies on all sides. Fight or die from turn one.

### Burning Building
The building you had chosen to reside in has suddenly caught fire! You might want to leave. Start in a structure that's actively on fire. Immediate evacuation puzzle — grab what you can and get out before you burn.

### Crazy Party
You'd been unsurprised when your vacation — a hastily arranged holiday to a remote resort to maintain distance from the riots — had been put out of its misery when state-wide evacuation was ordered after weeks of worsening violence, and the complex had been placed on a shortlist for... Resort/party location start. Possibly good initial supplies (food, drink) but weird starting position.

### Squatter
You have survived the initial wave of panic, and have achieved (relative) safety in an abandoned building. Unfortunately, while you're the only one here now, you are definitely not the first person to be here, and in its current state this building no longer seems nearly as secure as one would hope. Abandoned building. Previously looted, compromised security. You got the leftovers.

### Large Building
Whether due to stubbornness, ignorance, or just plain bad luck, you missed the evacuation and are stuck in a large building full of the risen dead. Big structure (mall, hospital, school?) crawling with zombies. Must fight or sneak your way out of a complex interior.

### Low Profile
You heard the global, worsening news and saw the nightmare in the cities firsthand — a situation that prompted you to jump from low-rent motel to cheap trailer, trying to outrun the madness. While only you know where you'd planned to go, when you heard the government's last broadcast and... Nomadic pre-Cataclysm lifestyle. Start in a motel/trailer. Some supplies from hopping between locations.

### Mass Quarantine
When FEMA camps ran out of space the authorities used what was left of their manpower to round up people at gunpoint. The stadiums they forced everyone into did nothing to slow down the madness and it spread like wildfire. You managed to slip away from the worst violence but you are... Stadium/camp start. Mass death site. Escaped a government quarantine gone wrong.

### Refugee Center
You have survived the initial wave of panic, and have made your way to a refugee center. Start at an NPC hub. More social options — traders, quests, faction NPCs. Safer start but more complex social dynamics.

### Missed
Whether due to stubbornness, ignorance, or just plain bad luck, you missed the evacuation and are stuck in a city full of the risen dead. Urban start surrounded by zombies. No shelter prepared. The city is the dungeon.

### The Mascot Rises
Business had been bad — for some reason, weeks of rioting, an evacuation order, and the sky breaking with alien shadows prompted a downturn in foot traffic, even among Foodplace's chain restaurants. As an employee, upon evacuation being declared, you disregarded company... Fast food employee who stayed behind. Comedy scenario. Start in a Foodplace restaurant. Foodplace uniform, access to kitchen supplies.

### Wilderness
You find yourself among trees. The screaming and moaning is fainter this far from civilization, but you'd better know what you're doing out here. Pure wilderness start. No buildings, no roads. Primitive survival from the jump.

### Golden Years
After a lifetime of hard work, you managed to settle down in a posh retirement community. You saw the riots on the news but assumed they would have no reason to attack some harmless old folks. That assumption was proven wrong by a group of lunatics breaking in, leaving you no... Elderly character at a retirement home. Likely stat penalties for age. The comfortable life is violently over.

### Surrounded in the Dark
You were camping in the wild to let things back home cool off. One night strange noises closed in on you and you were attacked. You managed to get away but can feel things moving in the dark. Nighttime wilderness start surrounded by threats you can't see. Immediate survival in darkness.

### Lone Survivor
You are the only one that made it. Everyone else died nearby. You have to get out of here! Corpses of your group around you. Solo start with whatever they were carrying. Grab and go.

### Safe Place
You've found a safe place away from the cities and devoid of the living dead. Looks like you're on your own... Remote safe location with no immediate threats. Peaceful start but isolated. Long-term survival challenge, not immediate combat.

### Middle of Nowhere
During your escape you got lost and ended up at the wrong location. Get your bearings and make a plan. You'll need it. Random spawn in an unfamiliar area. No plan, no preparation, no idea where you are.

### The Last Delivery
As the world was falling apart around you, something shook you out of your stupor: a phone call on Foodplace's excellent delivery service line. It was the most incoherent babbling you've ever heard, but it was undeniably the biggest order you've ever seen in your years working at... Companion scenario to The Mascot Rises. You're making a delivery during the apocalypse. Start with a vehicle and food.

### Last Flight
Once it became clear that the world was ending you took to the skies, hoping that your flying skills could lead you to safety. You found no standing safe zones, and when your fuel ran out, were forced to land in open field. Crash-landed in a field. Had access to aviation — possibly some gear. Now grounded in the middle of nowhere.

### Ambush
It is the winter after zero hour. As you were scavenging for food and a warm place to stay, you heard the sound of lots of movement nearby. Late-game start — winter, post-Cataclysm. Cold, hungry, and about to be mobbed. Requires achievement "A time to every purpose under heaven."

## Challenge Starts

### Challenge - Hunted Forever
Since the start of the Cataclysm, they have been hunting you relentlessly. You can try to fight them, but each time one falls another takes its place. The only escape is for you to die. Infinite hostile pursuers. Permanent chase. Pure survival timer — you're already dead, the question is how long.

### Challenge - Hunted
Since the start of the Cataclysm, something has been hunting you relentlessly. You can outrun it, but it always seems to find you eventually. The only escape is for one of you to die. Single relentless pursuer. Unlike Hunted Forever, this one can be killed — but it's powerful. You must eventually turn and fight.

### Challenge - Bordered
You have survived the initial wave of panic, and have achieved (relative) safety in one of the many government evac shelters. While evacuating, you noticed a mysterious sky-high wall in the distance, seemingly cutting off travel in every direction around you. Evacuee start but the world has physical boundaries. Constrained map. Resources within the walls are all you get.

### Challenge - Car Crash
You decided the open road would be a better prospect than the zombie-filled cities. While en route to your destination, you were involved in a crash that totaled your vehicle. Injured and alone, you'll need to think fast if you want to survive. Start injured on a road with a wrecked car. No shelter, no supplies beyond what's in the wreck. Immediate survival pressure.

### Challenge - Hunted by the Dead
Since the start of the Cataclysm, the air around you has felt different, like you pissed off whatever is causing the Cataclysm. Now zombie hordes will approach you everywhere you go, regardless of whether they see you or not. Permanent zombie magnet. Stealth is useless — they always know where you are. Constant pressure.

### Challenge - Lost Underground
When the riots came to your home, you ran underground and hid. Now days later you are lost and hear movement echoing in the dark. Start underground — sewers, basement, subway. Must navigate up to the surface while avoiding whatever's down there with you.

### Challenge - Fungal Infection
You feel spores crawling beneath your skin. It's only a matter of time. Start with fungal infection — a different death clock than zombie infection. The fungus transforms you rather than kills you. Requires achievement "Oh Shitake!"

### Challenge - Really Bad Day
You start drunk to the point of incapacitation, depressed, infected, surrounded by fire, and sick with the flu. This day went downhill really fast. Everything wrong simultaneously. Pure chaos. Requires achievement.

### Challenge - Medieval Peasant
"Som nigromancie hath brought yow hidder! Ye have only the hoose on youre legges and the knyf at youre syde and youre prayeres to Marie moder of God." Middle English flavor text. Start with medieval gear only — no modern technology comprehension. A peasant from the past dropped into the zombie apocalypse. Requires achievement.

### Challenge - Abandoned
Sickly and frail, you have spent most of your life in the patient's ward of various hospitals. When your current one was evacuated, you were considered a lost cause and left behind. You awaken to the sound of movement around the hospital. Hospital patient. Weak stats, medical conditions. The staff left you to die. Start in a hospital full of zombies.

### Challenge - Mi-Go Camp
You have found yourself in the most alien place you've ever seen. The hot, humid air and the organic appearance of the structure make you feel like you're trapped inside a giant creature. Whatever it is, you need to get out of here before They find you. Start inside an alien hive structure. Mi-Go are extradimensional insectoid horrors. Must escape a non-human environment with non-human threats.

### Challenge - Portal Dependent
You were caught in a huge and unstable portal storm, and were utterly unprotected from the full force of its effects. You're on the tail end of the storm now, and while you've survived, you feel... different. Start mutated by portal energy. Changed in ways you don't understand. Possible powers, possible liabilities.

### Challenge - Trans-Coast Logistics Concourse
You were deemed an essential employee and required to stay behind during the facility's evacuation. You'd better find your lanyard with your badge before the automated defenses notice that you don't have one. Corporate facility with automated security. You need your badge or the turrets will kill you. Puzzle-escape scenario.

### Challenge - Trans-Coast Logistics Mutant
You were an experimental subject confined to a government black site. While you were luckier than your fellow mutants, remaining somewhat human, you still bear many scars of the scientists' experiments. Unfortunately, the facility has fallen... Government black site. You're a mutant test subject. The facility is compromised. Escape with whatever mutations they gave you.

### Challenge - Island Prison
You were convicted or working in a high-security prison before the Cataclysm. Unfortunately, you're still in it, so you need to figure out how to escape. It's located on a remote island to boot; you'll eventually need to escape that also. Two-stage escape: first the prison, then the island. Water crossing required. Unique geography challenge.

### Challenge - Mansion Escape
You were living the high life when the world ended, surrounded by riches and eating well every day. Now you've woken up, alone and drugged, in a room of your mansion that you don't recognize. As you fight to remain conscious, you hear heavy footsteps nearby. Time to get the hell out of... Drugged in your own mansion. Something is in the house. Impaired start, horror atmosphere. Escape from inside.

### Challenge - Close Encounter
You don't know what it was but you managed to lose it for the time being. Now you are injured, surrounded and the building is on fire. You hope you have some time before it returns to finish the job. Something alien attacked you. Injured, burning building, surrounded, and it's coming back. Multiple simultaneous crises.

### Challenge - Shattered Sky
From up here, you watched it all fall apart. The sky cracked and indescribable alien shapes writhed in the clouds. The horizon has returned to normal, but you still see shadows moving in the corner of your eye and feel them calling you. Rooftop or high-altitude start. Witnessed the dimensional breach directly. Psychological effects. Something is calling to you.

### Challenge - Stronger Portal Storms
You've managed to get to a shelter, but reality has grown weaker than normal, and portal storms will be quite strong. Evacuee-like start but portal storms hit harder. Reality warps are a constant threat. Requires achievement "Reality is falling apart."

### Challenge - Frequent Portal Storms
You've managed to get to a shelter, but reality has grown weaker than normal, and portal storms will be more frequent. Similar to Stronger but frequency over intensity. Portal storms interrupt constantly. Requires same achievement.

### Challenge - Splintering Reality
You found yourself alone in an open field when reality began to heave and crack all around you. You need to find shelter, and fast. Open field with active portal storm. No shelter. Reality is breaking around you. Sprint for cover. Requires achievement.

### Challenge - Unliving History
New England is dotted with old forts from the Revolutionary War, and many of them are still standing. When things started getting really bad, it seemed like a natural place to hole up for a while, and it turns out that many, many other people had the same idea. The peace didn't last long. Revolutionary War fort. Overcrowded, then overrun. Historical setting meets zombie apocalypse.

### Challenge - Between a Rock and a Hard Place
You were trying to scavenge for supplies, and now some rough group of bandits has dragged monsters in with them where you were hiding! Caught between human bandits and monsters. Three-way conflict. No allies on either side.

### Challenge - Compromised Shelter
You have survived the initial wave of panic, and have achieved (relative) safety in one of the many government evac shelters. However, the people you came with started to turn and now they're out for your flesh! You've holed up in the basement for now, but sooner or later you have to make for... Evac shelter gone wrong. Your fellow evacuees are now zombies above you. Basement start, must fight up and out.

### Challenge - Last Stand
Together with some others you decided to take up arms against the horde. Now you are reconsidering whether that was a wise choice. Armed group fighting zombies. You're in the thick of combat. Allies present but the situation is deteriorating. Requires achievement "Decamate."

### Challenge - Bottom of a Mine
Start deep underground in a mine. Must navigate up to the surface.

### Challenge - Emergency Shutdown
Facility emergency scenario. Details require achievement to view.

### Challenge - Overrun
Overrun defensive position. Details require achievement to view.

### Challenge - Aircraft Carrier
Naval vessel start. Isolated at sea on a military ship.

## Special Starts

### Experiment
You have been heavily experimented upon for the advancement of genetic science, willingly or not. Once the Cataclysm struck, you escaped the lab and wandered aimlessly, ending up in a forest. Start with mutations from lab experiments. Could be powerful or debilitating depending on what they gave you. Wilderness start with a modified body.

### Strange Castle
You have survived the initial wave of panic, and made your way to the wilderness. You were expecting this area to be uninhabited, but instead you see a large castle guarded by strange robots. At the entrance stands a sign, it reads "Traders Come Well Avaunt." Robot-guarded castle in the wilderness. Trading post? Trap? The sign is in archaic English. Requires achievement.

### The Next Summer
Late start, summer after the Cataclysm. The world has had time to decay and evolve. Requires achievement.

### A Moonlight Night
Nighttime scenario. Requires achievement.

### Brave New World
Post-apocalyptic late start. The world has moved on. Requires achievement.
