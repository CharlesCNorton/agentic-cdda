# Stats

Four primary stats, each defaulting to 8. Points are shared across all four. Range is typically 4-14 at character creation.

## Strength (STR)
Raw physical power. Affects:
- **Base HP:** Each point of STR adds to your hit point pool (85 HP at STR 8)
- **Carry weight:** How much you can haul before being encumbered (99.2 lbs at STR 8)
- **Knockdown resistance:** Higher STR means harder to knock down (8.0 at STR 8)
- **Intimidation skill:** Used in NPC dialogue intimidation checks (16 at STR 8)
- **Maximum oxygen:** How long you can hold your breath (46 at STR 8)
- **Shout volume:** How far your yells carry (26 at STR 8)
- **Lifting strength:** What you can pick up and move
- **Swim speed:** Move cost while swimming (360 at STR 8)
- **Bash damage bonus:** Extra melee damage from raw force (6.0 at STR 8)
- **Throwing range, accuracy, and damage**
- **Reload speed for muscle-powered weapons** (bows, crossbows)

STR is the survival stat. Low STR means you can't carry supplies, can't hit hard, can't break through doors, and drown faster. High STR means you're a pack mule who hits like a truck but may lack finesse. For a blind agent, STR matters less than in visual play because you won't be optimizing melee positioning — but carry weight and HP always matter.

## Dexterity (DEX)
Agility, coordination, fine motor control. Affects:
- **Melee to-hit bonus:** Accuracy in close combat (+0.00 at DEX 8)
- **Throwing penalty per target dodge:** How much enemy dodging hurts your throws (+25 at DEX 8)
- **Ranged penalty:** Accuracy penalty for guns and thrown weapons (-6 at DEX 8)
- **Dodge skill:** Ability to avoid incoming attacks (4 at DEX 8)
- **Swim speed:** Combined with STR for swimming
- **Lockpicking effectiveness**
- **Grab resistance:** Chance of escaping monster grabs
- **Trap escape:** Chance of getting out of bear traps, pits, etc.
- **Trap disarming:** Effectiveness at neutralizing traps
- **Gun modification:** Success chance when modifying weapons

DEX is the combat stat. It determines whether you hit, whether you dodge, and whether you escape when grabbed. For a blind agent, DEX is critical — dodge is your primary defense when you can't see enemies coming and can't manually kite them around obstacles.

## Intelligence (INT)
Mental acuity, learning speed, problem solving. Affects:
- **Read times:** How fast you get through books (100% at INT 8)
- **Persuade/lie skill:** Used in NPC dialogue checks (16 at INT 8)
- **Crafting bonus:** Quality improvement on crafted items (8% at INT 8)
- **Practical experience catch-up:** Speed of converting book knowledge into real skill
- **Trap detection and disarming**
- **Bionic installation success:** Chance of successful CBM surgery
- **Gun modification success**
- **Recipe learning:** Chance to memorize recipes while crafting from books
- **Martial arts technique learning**

INT is the long-game stat. Low INT means you read slowly, craft poorly, and fail bionic installations. High INT means you power through books, master crafting early, and succeed at complex technical tasks. For a blind agent relying on text descriptions and NPC dialogue, INT directly improves persuasion checks and crafting — both text-native activities.

## Perception (PER)
Senses, awareness, attention to detail. Affects:
- **Aiming penalty:** How quickly you can aim ranged weapons (-14 at PER 8)
- **Persuade/lie skill:** Combined with INT for dialogue (16 at PER 8)
- **Practical experience catch-up:** Like INT, affects learning speed
- **Safe cracking speed**
- **Sight distance:** How far you can see on the game map and overmap
- **Stealing effectiveness**
- **Throwing accuracy**
- **Vehicle control:** Chance of losing control while driving
- **Spotting camouflaged creatures**
- **Lockpicking effectiveness**

PER is the awareness stat. In visual play, high PER means you spot threats early and aim fast. For a blind agent, PER is paradoxically both less useful (can't leverage sight distance visually) and more useful (the look/examine system likely keys off PER for what you can detect). The dialogue bonus stacks with INT, making high INT+PER a strong talker build.

## Point Economy
All four stats start at 8. You have a limited pool to redistribute. Raising one stat above 8 costs increasingly more points; lowering one below 8 frees points. The tradeoffs:

- **Combat build:** High STR + DEX, lower INT + PER. Hit hard, dodge well, but slow learner.
- **Scholar build:** High INT + PER, lower STR + DEX. Fast learner, good crafter, weak in combat.
- **Balanced:** All 8s or 9s. No weaknesses, no strengths. Safe but unexciting.
- **Min-max:** Dump one stat to 4, max another to 14. Powerful in one dimension, crippled in another.

For a blind text-only agent, the optimal build likely favors INT + PER (dialogue, crafting, learning) over STR + DEX (combat, carry weight), since text-based play naturally gravitates toward NPC interaction, reading, and crafting rather than precise melee positioning.
